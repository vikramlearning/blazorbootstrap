# E-commerce Storefront

Kindred Goods is a fictitious home goods store. Shoppers browse and filter the catalog, read and write reviews, fill a cart, and check out with a test card; customers with an account track orders, request returns, and keep addresses and a wishlist. The retailer runs the store from a separate back office: fulfilment, products and stock, prices and coupons, customers, returns, review moderation, and sales analytics.

## Roles and demo accounts

| Role | Demo account | Password | Start page |
|:--|:--|:--|:--|
| Customer | Maya Lindqvist (`maya@kindredgoods.example`) | `shop-demo-24` | `/showcase/ecommerce/account` |
| Retailer | Owen Hart (`owen@kindredgoods.example`) | `store-demo-24` | `/showcase/ecommerce/admin` |

You can also register ("Create an account" in the account menu, or `?mode=register` on the sign-in page). A new account is a customer. You don't need an account to shop: a visitor can check out as a guest.

Maya starts with past orders, two saved addresses in the same-day delivery area, and a wishlist with one product in stock and one out of stock (with a back-in-stock notice requested).

All data is fictitious and there is no backend. Each browser tab keeps its own data in `sessionStorage` (it survives a reload; other tabs start fresh), and "Reset demo data" in the account menu restores the seed. The sign-in page lists the demo accounts with one-click buttons; credentials are checked only against the tab's accounts. The clock starts at November 9, 2026, 10:00 and moves on with real time. Checkout accepts only the published test cards (for example `4242 4242 4242 4242`; `4000 0000 0000 0002` is declined and `4000 0000 0000 9995` fails for insufficient funds), with any future expiry.

## What to try

1. As a visitor, filter the catalog by category, price, and rating, add products from the cards or a product page, and open the cart drawer. On the cart page, apply `WELCOME10` (10% off a first order), then check out with `4242 4242 4242 4242`. Try the decline card first: the cart stays as it was.
2. Sign in as Maya and check out to her saved Fairbank address with same-day local delivery: a map of the courier's route and the estimated arrival appear. `WELCOME10` is refused, since Maya has ordered before.
3. Sign in as Owen. In **Orders**, use the fulfilment board to pack the new order, ship it with a tracking number, and mark it delivered. Sign in as Maya: each step is a notice on her account, and the order page shows the timeline.
4. As Maya, request a return on a delivered order. As Owen, approve it in **Returns**: the items go back into stock and the amount is refunded.
5. As Owen, open **Products**, give the Stoneware Bud Vase (out of stock) some stock. The store shows the change at once, and Maya gets a back-in-stock notice for the vase on her wishlist. A sale started in **Pricing** shows in the store the same way.
6. As Owen, choose **View store**: a bar says you are viewing the store as the retailer, and the cart and checkout are not offered to staff.

| Product | `/showcase/ecommerce/products/{Id:int}` | Images, rating, price, color and quantity, add to cart, wishlist heart, tabs for details, reviews (write, edit, delete your own), and shipping, and related products. |

## How the theme is built

This is one of the older sites, so there is no shared frame component. The storefront layout, `EcommerceLayout.razor`, has the root `.sc-site.sc-ecommerce`; the back office, `EcommerceAdminLayout.razor`, has `.sc-site.sc-ecommerce.ea-admin`. Scoped CSS ties each layout's variables to its own elements, so `EcommerceAdminLayout.razor.css` repeats the store's color tokens (with a slightly darker page background, `#f7f4f0`). The admin has its own layout with the modules in a sidebar, separate from the storefront (D-031). When the retailer views the store, a "Viewing your store as Owen Hart (Retailer)" bar with "Back to admin" sits above the header, and buying is hidden (D-032).

Color tokens in light mode: `--sc-primary` `#c2410c` (burnt orange), `--sc-primary-hover` `#9a3412`, contrast `#fff`, page background `#fdfaf6`. In dark mode (`[data-bs-theme=dark]`): `--sc-primary` `#fb923c`, hover `#fdba74`, contrast `#1c1009`, page background `#16110d`. Because the dark primary is a light orange, `.text-bg-primary` text switches to the dark contrast color. Sale prices and stars have their own tokens (`--ec-sale`, `--ec-star`).

Fonts come from Google Fonts: Fraunces (600 and 700) for headings and DM Sans for body text.

The shared stylesheet `wwwroot/css/blazorbootstrap.demo.rcl.css` maps the `--sc-*` tokens to Bootstrap (`--bs-primary`, link and focus colors, body and heading fonts, primary buttons). There is no chart theme class: the analytics page sets its chart colors itself. The series colors are mid-tones that keep 3:1 contrast on both the light and the dark panel (store orange `#ea580c`, and one color per order status), and the axis, grid, and legend colors follow the color mode. When you switch the theme, the admin's site bar tells `EcommerceState`, and the charts redraw.

## Accessibility and responsiveness

- The quantity stepper's minus and plus buttons stay enabled at the limits, so keyboard focus never drops; a press past a limit does nothing.
- The wishlist heart uses `aria-pressed` and announces the change; star ratings on cards are icons with a text alternative, so a grid of cards adds no extra tab stops.
- Each analytics chart has a visually hidden data table, and a status region announces the chosen range.
- The account tabs mark the current page with `aria-current="page"` and a filled pill, not color alone. Dialogs return focus to the button that opened them, or to the status message when that button is gone.
- At 375 px the catalog filters fold behind a "Show filters" button (with the number of active filters), the header collapses into a menu, and the account tabs wrap.
