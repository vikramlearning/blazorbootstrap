# HR & Employee Portal

Teamhaven is the people portal of Brightfield Robotics, a fictitious company of about 190 people in 7 departments and 3 locations. Employees find colleagues in the directory and the org chart, request leave against their balances, tick off their onboarding checklist, and write their self review. Managers approve or reject their reports' leave, follow new hires, and review their team. HR admins manage employees, leave policies, onboarding templates, and headcount analytics, run performance review cycles, and read the audit log.

The portal's record lists (employees, leave approvals, the team, policies, templates, reviews, and the audit log) are [Grid](/components/grid) lists with a saved view, a column chooser, and CSV export; create and edit forms validate with a linked error summary; and the header has a global search and notifications. Every change lands in an audit log. The signature workflow is the **performance review cycle**: HR opens a cycle for some departments or the whole company, each employee writes a self review, the manager writes a review with a rating, HR calibrates the ratings across the group, and the employee and the manager sign off. Each person sees what is waiting for them and the cycle's progress.

3. As Theo, open **My onboarding** and tick the overdue security training. As Rafael, **New hires** shows Theo's progress; tick his two-week check-in there.
4. Sign in as Ingrid, open **Employees**, and add a new hire who reports to Rafael: the work email is checked for uniqueness as you type, and the probation end date must come after the start date. 6. As Ingrid, raise Vacation's yearly days in **Leave policies** (the editor opens from a row). As Theo, the balance on **My leave** follows.
7. Use the search box in the header: type a colleague's name, a leave request number like `#4`, or (as Ingrid) a policy, template, or review cycle name, and open a match.
8. As Ingrid, open **Audit log**: every request, decision, change, and review step from the steps above is there, with who did it and when.

### Walk through a performance review cycle

1. **Self review (employee).** Sign in as Theo. **Home** shows "Reviews waiting for you", and the bell says his self review is waiting. Open it (or **My reviews**), write at least 50 characters, optionally rate yourself, and submit. The review page shows the steps and that Rafael writes his review next.
2. **Manager review (manager).** Sign in as Rafael. The bell says Theo's self review is in, and **Team reviews** marks Theo, Leila, and Mei "Waiting for you". Open Theo's review, read his self review, write your review, choose a rating, and submit. Do the same for Leila and Mei.
3. **Manager reviews for managers who can't sign in (HR).** Sign in as Ingrid and open **Performance reviews**, then the Engineering and Product cycle. The progress shows how many reviews are in; managers without a sign-in are marked "No sign-in: HR records". Open one of their reports' reviews from the **People** grid to record a review for the manager, if you like.
4. **Calibration (HR).** Open the **Sales** cycle, which is already in calibration. The **Rating distribution** table compares the departments. Choose **Calibrate** on a row: keep the rating, or change it and say why (a note is required when the rating changes). Choose **Release for sign-off**: the dialog says how many ratings aren't calibrated and keep the manager's, and everyone who can sign in is notified.
5. **Moving a cycle on with reviews missing (HR).** Open the Engineering and Product cycle and choose **Move to calibration**. The dialog counts and names the reviews still without a manager review; confirming leaves them out of calibration and sign-off. Calibrate, then release it for sign-off.
6. **Sign-off (employee and manager).** As Theo, the bell says your review is ready: open it, read Rafael's review and your calibrated rating with HR's note, add an optional comment, and sign off. As Rafael, sign off Theo's review from **Team reviews** (or the bell). In the Sales cycle, managers can't sign in, so Ingrid records their sign-offs from each review.
7. **Close (HR).** As Ingrid, choose **Close the cycle**: the dialog names who hasn't signed off yet. The cycle shows as Closed in **Performance reviews**, and **Audit log** lists every step.
8. **Open a new cycle (HR).** In **Performance reviews**, choose **Open a review cycle**. Choose **Some departments**, tick Design, People, and Finance and operations, and pick the due dates: the preview says how many employees will be included and who isn't (joining, or with no manager). A department whose cycle isn't closed yet can't be chosen: the preview and the form name the cycle that covers it. Open the cycle: everyone in it who can sign in is notified.

## Performance review cycle

A cycle moves through four statuses, and HR moves it from one to the next:

1. **Open.** HR opens a cycle with a unique name, a scope (the whole company, or some departments), and two due dates: self reviews after today, and manager reviews after the self reviews and within 180 days. Everyone in scope who works here, has started, and has a manager takes part; the manager they have that day writes and signs their review. The chief executive, with no manager, isn't reviewed in the portal, and people joining later aren't included. Only one cycle that isn't closed may cover a department. Each employee writes a self review (50 to 2,000 characters, with an optional own rating) while the cycle is open and before the manager's review. The manager writes a review (50 to 2,000 characters) with a rating once the self review is in, or without it when the employee can't sign in or the self-review due date has passed. Ratings run from 1 to 5: Below expectations, Partly meets expectations, Meets expectations, Exceeds expectations, and Exceptional. A review can't be changed once submitted. The employee doesn't see the manager's review until the release.
2. **Calibration.** HR moves the cycle on once every manager review is in, or after confirming who is still missing one: those reviews are left out of calibration and sign-off. HR calibrates any rating (not their own review); a changed rating needs a note of 10 to 300 characters, which the employee and the manager read after the release. The rating distribution by department shows whether each department rates by the same bar.
3. **Sign-off.** HR releases the cycle; ratings nobody calibrated keep the manager's. The employee reads the manager's review and the calibrated rating and signs off, with an optional comment of up to 500 characters; the manager signs off too.
4. **Closed.** HR closes the cycle once everyone has signed off, or after confirming who hasn't. A closed cycle is kept for the record.

For a manager who can't sign in to Teamhaven (most managers in the seed), the HR admin records the manager review and the manager's sign-off for them, and the audit log names both.

Each step notifies whoever must act next: everyone in a new cycle (their self review is waiting), the manager when a report's self review is in (or the HR admins, for a manager who can't sign in), the HR admins when every manager review is in and when everyone has signed off, each employee and manager when their sign-off is waiting, and the HR admins when there are manager sign-offs to record. Every step writes an audit entry: opening a cycle, each self and manager review, the move to calibration (with who was left out), each calibration, the release (with how many ratings kept the manager's), each sign-off, and the close (with who didn't sign).

"Reviews waiting for you" on **Home**, **My reviews**, **Team reviews**, **My team**, **Employees**, and **Performance reviews** lists each person's next steps.

## Enterprise features

- **Grid lists**: employees, leave approvals (waiting and decided), the team, leave policies, onboarding templates, your reviews, team reviews, review cycles, the people in a cycle, and the audit log use the [Grid](/components/grid) with sorting, a column chooser, and CSV export of the filtered rows; long lists page. Each list keeps its view (sort, page size, hidden and reordered columns) in the tab's `sessionStorage` until **Reset view**. A row opens its record through a stretched link, or a stretched button that opens the editor or the decision dialog where a record has no page.
- **Bulk actions**: the leave approvals queue approves or rejects the selected requests together, with one note for every employee (required to reject).
- **Validated forms**: required, length, and format rules; uniqueness checks with a "Checking…" state (employee work email, leave policy, template, and review cycle names); cross-field rules (the probation end after the start date, a leave request's dates in order with enough notice, days a year unless there's no yearly limit, a new hire's task not due before day one, review due dates in order, a note when HR changes a rating); and a summary that links each failing field after a failed submit.
- **Global search** in the header: people by name, email, or title for everyone; your own leave requests and reviews; your reports' requests for managers, and everyone's for HR; leave policies, onboarding templates, and review cycles for HR.
- **Notifications**: new and changed leave requests for whoever decides them, decisions for the employee, onboarding started, a new report or a report who left, a newly registered account for HR, and each review step, with an unread count and mark as read.
- **Audit log**: every create, change, decision, delete, and review step, with who did it, when, and what changed.
- **Loading, empty, and error states** on every list, with a "Demo: simulate a loading error" control to show the error state and its Retry.

## How the theme is built

`HrFrame.razor` is the frame both layouts share: `HrLayout` (the public start page and sign-in, with a Navbar) and `HrWorkLayout` (the signed-in portal, with a top bar and a Sidebar). Its root is `.sc-site.sc-hr`, and `HrFrame.razor.css` styles the pages of both through `::deep`, including the Grid lists, the bulk bar, and the status regions.

Color tokens in light mode: `--sc-primary` `#7e22ce` (purple), `--sc-primary-hover` `#6b21a8`, contrast `#fff`, page background `#f8f6fb`. In dark mode (`[data-bs-theme=dark]`): `--sc-primary` `#d8b4fe`, hover `#e9d5ff`, contrast `#2a0f45`, page background `#110c18`. Because the dark primary is light, `.text-bg-primary` and `.progress-bar` text switch to the dark contrast color. The sidebar sets the `--bb-sidebar-*` variables to `#2a0f45` with light text in both modes.

Fonts come from Google Fonts: Nunito Sans (400 to 800) for body and headings, and JetBrains Mono for the figures in tables.

The shared stylesheet `wwwroot/css/blazorbootstrap.demo.rcl.css` maps the `--sc-*` tokens to Bootstrap (`--bs-primary`, link and focus colors, body and heading fonts, primary buttons). Charts draw on a canvas, so `HrChartTheme.cs` holds their colors: series `#7e22ce`, `#047857`, `#b45309`, `#0369a1`, `#be185d` in light mode and `#d8b4fe`, `#34d399`, `#fbbf24`, `#60a5fa`, `#f472b6` in dark mode, plus axis, grid, legend, and tooltip colors. When you switch the theme, the site bar tells `HrState`, and the charts redraw.

Following the shared pattern for business roles, there is one signed-in portal with a sidebar instead of role links in the public header. Each role page has its own role guard, and `HrState` checks the role (and the relationship, such as being the employee's manager) on every action. Leave is counted in working days (weekdays that aren't company holidays), and balances run per calendar year.

## Accessibility and responsiveness

- Every request, decision, review step, tick, and refusal is announced in a status region, and errors in alert regions. Focus returns to the button that opened a dialog, or moves to the announcement after a save.
- Each leave approval checkbox is labelled with its request ("Select Leila Haddad's Vacation, Dec 21–23, 2026"), and the header checkbox reads "Select all shown requests"; sortable headers carry `aria-sort`. Visually hidden button text keeps its leading space, so buttons read "Edit Vacation", not "EditVacation".
- After a failed submit, the error summary takes focus and links to each field; each message is tied to its field with `aria-describedby`.
- The org chart mind map works with the keyboard and has a find box; the same chart is repeated as a nested list, and the department highlight is announced with a count.
- Each chart has a text summary and a data table; the rating distribution is a table with counts and shares.
- Leave statuses, employee statuses ("Joining Dec 1", "Left Aug 28"), review stages, cycle statuses, and ratings ("4 · Exceeds expectations") are written in words, not shown by color alone. A review's steps say which is done, current, or waiting.
- At 375 px the sidebar sits above the page, the review steps and bulk bar wrap, and wide tables scroll sideways.
