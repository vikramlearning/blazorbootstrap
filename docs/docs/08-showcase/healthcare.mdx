# Healthcare Clinic Portal

Cedarline Health is a fictitious network of three family clinics (Downtown, Lakeview, and Northfield) with six doctors. Patients find a doctor, book and change visits, read visit summaries and lab reports (with a PDF), ask for refills, and pay bills. Doctors run their visits, charts, lab results, and refill requests, and the front desk keeps the calendar, checks patients in, and sets doctors' hours. Nothing on the site is medical advice.

You can also create an account ("Register as a patient" on the start page). A new account is a patient with an empty record, so you can book right away.

Finding a doctor, doctor profiles, and locations are public. The other five doctors and seven other patients can't sign in.

All data is fictitious and there is no backend. Each browser tab keeps its own data in `sessionStorage` (it survives a reload; other tabs start fresh), and "Reset demo data" in the account menu restores the seed: 8 patients, 17 appointments (today's clinic days, past visits, and upcoming ones), 5 lab orders, 4 prescriptions, and 4 bills. The sign-in page lists the demo accounts with one-click buttons; credentials are checked only against the tab's accounts. The clock starts on Tuesday, November 24, 2026, 8:20, before the first visit of the day, and moves on with real time.

Bills are paid with a test card form that accepts only these published test numbers, with any future expiry and any CVC (4 digits for American Express). Only the brand and last four digits are kept.

## What to try

1. Without signing in, open **Find a doctor**, open Dr. Okoye's profile, and pick one of today's open times. Sign in as Maya when asked, give a reason, and book. Then sign in as Lena: **Check-in** lists the new visit.
2. As Lena, check Maya in. Sign in as Dr. Okoye: **My schedule** shows her as checked in and waiting. **Start visit**, write notes and a summary, order a lab test, prescribe, and **Complete the visit**. As Maya, the appointment shows the summary (never the staff notes), the prescription, and a new bill.
3. As Dr. Okoye, open **Lab results**, enter Maya's HbA1c, and release it (each released test bills $25.00). As Maya, open **Lab reports**: the report shows the values with flags in words, the doctor's comment, and the PDF with a download link.
4. As Maya, open **Prescriptions** and request a refill of metformin (it has none left). As Dr. Okoye, approve it in **Refill requests**. Maya sees the answer and one refill left.
5. As Lena, open **Doctors' hours** and add a day off for Dr. Okoye. His public profile then shows him away that day, with no open times.
6. As Maya, open **Bills** and pay the dermatology bill with `4242 4242 4242 4242`. Try `4000 0000 0000 0002` first to see a decline.

## Components used

- [PDF Viewer](/components/pdf-viewer): each lab report, which the site writes as a one-page PDF (`HcLabPdf.cs`) and shows from a data URL.
- [Google Map](/components/google-map): the clinics on Locations. Without a `GoogleMap:ApiKey` setting the site shows a drawn map preview with the same numbered pins.
- [Tabs](/components/tabs): the doctor's patient chart.
- [Modal](/components/modal): cancel an appointment, complete a visit, release a lab result, stop a prescription, the calendar's visit actions, mark a no-show, and delete hours or time off.
- [Sidebar](/components/sidebar): the staff back office, with the doctor's or the front desk's modules.
- [Navbar](/components/navbar): the patient portal header.
- [Breadcrumb](/components/breadcrumb): the booking, appointment, and lab report pages.
- [Alert](/components/alerts): a PDF that fails to load.
- [Input Group](/components/input-group): the test card form on Bills.
- [Buttons](/components/buttons) and [Icons](/content/icons): actions and icons across the site.
- [Text Input](/forms/text-input), [Avatar](/components/avatar), [Dropdown](/components/dropdown), and [Theme Switcher](/components/theme-switcher): through the shared sign-in, profile, account menu, and site bar.

The booking times are toggle buttons in the site's `SlotPicker` component; other form fields are native inputs and selects. The site has no charts.

## How the theme is built

`HcFrame.razor` is the frame both layouts share: `HcLayout` (the public and patient portal, with a Navbar) and `HcStaffLayout` (the staff back office, with a top bar, "View patient portal", and a Sidebar). Its root is `.sc-site.sc-health`, and `HcFrame.razor.css` styles the pages of both through `::deep`.

Color tokens in light mode: `--sc-primary` `#0e7490` (teal), `--sc-primary-hover` `#155e75`, contrast `#fff`, page background `#f4f8fa`. In dark mode (`[data-bs-theme=dark]`): `--sc-primary` `#67e8f9`, hover `#a5f3fc`, contrast `#062a33`, page background `#0a1519`. Because the dark primary is light, `.text-bg-primary` and `.progress-bar` text switch to the dark contrast color. The sidebar sets the `--bb-sidebar-*` variables to `#0b3440` with light text in both modes.

Fonts come from Google Fonts: Source Sans 3 (400, 600, 700) for body and headings, and JetBrains Mono for times, hours, and medical record numbers.

The shared stylesheet `wwwroot/css/blazorbootstrap.demo.rcl.css` maps the `--sc-*` tokens to Bootstrap (`--bs-primary`, link and focus colors, body and heading fonts, primary buttons).

The site follows the shared split between the public side and the back office: patients use the portal, staff work in a separate back office with a sidebar, and staff who open the portal get the "viewing as" bar. Each page has its own role guard, and `HcState` checks the role and ownership on every action. Patients get copies of their records without the staff notes, without a summary before the visit is completed, and without lab values before release.

## Accessibility and responsiveness

- Open times are buttons with `aria-pressed`, grouped by day, so booking and rescheduling work with the keyboard.
- Visit statuses and lab flags (such as "High") are written in words, not shown by color alone.
- The PDF viewer's toolbar is mouse-only, so every report also has a Download PDF link, and the results are on the page as a table.
- The map (or its preview) is described in words and the clinics are listed below it with matching numbers.
- Every check-in, booking, payment, and refusal is announced in a status region, and focus returns to the button that opened a dialog. At 375 px the staff sidebar sits above the page, the calendar's doctor columns stack, and wide tables scroll.
