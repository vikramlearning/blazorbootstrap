# Online Learning

Quillpath Academy is a fictitious online school with eight published courses and one draft. Learners enroll, work through video, reading, and quiz lessons, ask questions under a lesson, review courses, and earn certificates that anyone can verify. Instructors build their courses in a separate back office: details, modules, lessons with a rich-text editor, quizzes, publishing, the Q&A inbox, review replies, and the learner roster.

## What to try

1. Sign in as Jordan, open **My learning**, and continue Blazor from First Principles. Mark the reading lesson as complete, then take the quiz that follows: answer every question, submit, and read the score and the explanations. Passing completes the lesson.
2. Under a lesson, ask a question. Sign in as Avery: the account menu counts the question, and the **Q&A inbox** lists it. Answer it, then sign in as Jordan and read the answer under the lesson.
3. Open **Verify a certificate** and enter `QP-7K4M-R2XD`. The result names the learner, course, and issue date, and the URL (`?code=`) can be shared. Open the certificate from **Certificates** and print it.
4. As Avery, open **Courses** and publish the Accessible Blazor Components draft (its first module is ready; the second module's lessons are drafts you can publish from the course editor). Back in the school, the course is in the catalog, and a learner can enroll.
5. As Avery, edit a lesson with the rich-text editor and compare the preview with the lesson player, or open a quiz in the quiz builder and change the correct answer.
6. As Jordan, review Blazor from First Principles on its course page. As Avery, reply to the review or hide it in **Reviews**; Jordan's course page says so.

## How the theme is built

`LearningFrame.razor` is the frame both layouts share: `LearningLayout` (the school, with a Navbar) and `TeachLayout` (a teaching top bar with "View school" and a Sidebar). Its root is `.sc-site.sc-learning`, and `LearningFrame.razor.css` styles the pages of both through `::deep`. Teaching has its own layout, separate from the school (D-031). When an instructor opens the school, a "Viewing the school as Avery Calloway (Instructor)" bar with "Back to teaching" sits above the header; enrolling is for learners (D-032).

Color tokens in light mode: `--sc-primary` `#a16207` (amber), `--sc-primary-hover` `#854d0e`, contrast `#fff`, page background `#fbf8f1`. In dark mode (`[data-bs-theme=dark]`): `--sc-primary` `#facc15`, hover `#fde047`, contrast `#1c1400`, page background `#13110c`. Because the dark primary is a light yellow, `.text-bg-primary` and `.progress-bar` text switch to the dark contrast color.

Fonts come from Google Fonts: Lora (500 to 700) for headings and Source Sans 3 for body text.

The shared stylesheet `wwwroot/css/blazorbootstrap.demo.rcl.css` maps the `--sc-*` tokens to Bootstrap (`--bs-primary`, link and focus colors, body and heading fonts, primary buttons). Charts draw on a canvas, so `LearningChartTheme.cs` holds their colors. Unlike the other sites, the learning state doesn't track the color mode, so the charts don't redraw on a theme change: every color is picked to read on both the light panel (`#fff`) and the dark panel (`#1c1912`). The funnel stages are one amber hue stepped from light to dark (`#f59e0b`, `#d97706`, `#b45309`, `#92400e`), the rating bars are `#d97706`, and axis text is a neutral gray (`#7c7c84`).

## Accessibility and responsiveness

- Each quiz question is a `fieldset` with a `legend` and native radios. Submitting with a question unanswered names the missing ones and focuses the first; the score is announced, and each question says Right or Wrong in text.
- Modules and lessons are reordered with Move up and Move down buttons, not drag and drop; focus stays on the moved item, and its new position is announced.
- The PDF viewer's toolbar needs a mouse, so the handout is also a plain link ("Open the handout (PDF, new tab)").
- Each dashboard chart has a heading, a summary in words, a label, and a visually hidden data table. The lesson outline marks the current lesson with `aria-current`, and completion is said in text.
- At 375 px the Navbar collapses into a menu, and the lesson outline moves below the lesson. Certificates print without the site's chrome.
