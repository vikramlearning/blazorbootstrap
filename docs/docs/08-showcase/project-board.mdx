# Project & Client Board

Tandem is the project and client board of Brightline Studio, a fictitious agency. Team members work their tasks on Kanban boards and log their hours; project managers run projects, milestones, workload, the roadmap, and reports, and approve the hours on their projects; account managers keep clients and contacts, the deal pipeline, activity, and the forecast, and invoice the approved hours. Winning a deal creates a planned project, so the sales side and the delivery side meet in one place.

Every record list is a [Grid](/components/grid) with a saved view, a column chooser, and CSV export; forms validate with a linked error summary; the app's header has a global search and a notifications bell; and every change lands in an audit log. The signature workflow is **timesheets to client invoice**: team members log hours against projects and submit the week; each project's manager approves its hours or returns the week with a reason; approved billable hours become a draft invoice that the client's account manager checks, issues with payment terms, and marks paid.

## Timesheets and invoicing

**Logging hours.** A person logs hours on the projects they're on (or run) that have started and aren't planned; a completed project takes hours up to its due date. An entry is a day of the week, not in the future and not before the project's start; 0.25 to 12 hours in quarter hours, with at most 24 hours in a day; optionally one of the project's tasks; billable or not (billable by default on a client project, never on an internal one); and a note of up to 200 characters. A week runs Monday to Sunday and starts as a draft with its first entry.

**Submitting and approving.** A draft or returned week with at least one entry can be submitted (not a future week). Each project with hours in it then waits for its approver: the project's manager, or any project manager who can sign in when the project's manager can't. A week with hours on two projects needs both approvals; the week page and the timesheet show each project's state. Any approver can return the whole week with a reason of 10 to 300 characters: the person can change it again, and every approval is cleared, because any line may change before the week comes back. A submitted or approved week can't change.

**Rates.** Each person bills at the rate of the role their job title falls under: Design (designers and researchers), Engineering (developers, engineers, and anyone else), Quality assurance (QA), Content (content strategists and copywriters), or Management (project and account managers). The seed's rates are 140, 150, 120, 110, and 160 an hour, the same number in every billing currency. Account managers change them on Billing rates (50 to 400); new drafts use the new rates, and existing invoices keep theirs.

**Invoices.** Once every project in a week is approved, its billable hours are ready to invoice. The client's account manager makes a draft from the client's approved, uninvoiced billable hours up to a day (not in the future): one line per project and person, at the rate of the person's role, rounded to cents, in the client's billing currency. Removing a draft line, or deleting the draft, puts its hours back. Issuing a draft with at least one line gives it the next number of the year (like INV-2026-0074), today's date, and a due date by the payment terms (Net 14, 30, 45, or 60), and locks it. An issued invoice past its due date is overdue (a badge in words) until it is marked paid with a payment date from the issue date to today. A project with logged hours keeps its client and can't be deleted, and a client with invoices can't be deleted.

**Budget.** A project's board header and its client's page show the hours logged against the project's budget hours and the amount billed (issued and paid invoices) against its budget amount, with a warning in words at 80% and at 100%.

**Notifications.** A project's approver is told when a week with hours on it is submitted (or resubmitted); the person is told when a project's hours are approved, when the whole week is approved, and when it is returned (with the reason); the client's account manager is told when a week with billable hours on their client is approved ("ready to invoice"), and, when they sign in, about their clients' invoices that are overdue; a project's manager is told when its hours are invoiced.

**Audit.** "Week submitted" and "Week resubmitted", "Hours approved", "Week approved", "Week returned" (with the reason), entry changes in a returned week ("Hours logged", "Hours changed", "Hours deleted"), "Draft invoice created", "Invoice line removed", "Draft invoice deleted", "Invoice issued", "Invoice paid", and "Rate changed". A draft week's entries are the person's own work in progress and aren't audited until the week has been submitted.

## Enterprise features

- **Grid lists**: clients, projects (Project boards and Manage projects), My tasks, a client's contacts and deals, the timesheet's entries and weeks, approvals, ready to invoice, invoices, and the audit log use the [Grid](/components/grid) with sorting, paging, a column chooser, and CSV export of the filtered rows. Each list keeps its view (sort, page size, hidden and reordered columns) in the tab's `sessionStorage` until **Reset view**. A whole row opens its record (or, without a page of its own, its dialog). Clients, Manage projects, My tasks, and Approvals have multi-row selection with a bulk bar.
- **Validated forms**: the create and edit forms use `EditForm`: client, contact, project, milestone, task, comment, checklist item, deal, activity, time entry, return reason, draft invoice, issue, payment, rates, and work details. Required, length, and format rules (websites, emails, phone numbers, keys, quarter hours), uniqueness checks with a "Checking…" state (client name, project key, project name per client, contact email per client), cross-field rules (contract end after start, project due after start, money budget needs hours, task due within the project's dates, assignee on the project, close date not in the past, activity not in the future, hours within the week and 24 a day, nothing billable on an internal project, a payment not before the issue date), and a summary that links each failing field after a failed submit.
- **Global search** in the header: everyone finds projects (key, name, client) and tasks (key, title); account managers also find clients, contacts, and invoices by number.
- **Notifications** with an unread count and mark as read: tasks assigned to you, comments and @mentions on your tasks, tasks due today, tomorrow, or overdue, tasks ready for review, projects you now manage and their status changes, clients you now own and contacts added to them, projects from won deals, and every timesheet and invoice step above.
- **Audit log** of every create, change, move, assignment, completion, and delete (clients, contacts, projects, milestones, tasks, checklists, comments, deals, activity) and every timesheet and invoice step. The Activity page stays the account managers' client timeline.
- **Loading, empty, and error states** on every list, with a "Demo: simulate a loading error" control to show the error state and its Retry.

## How the theme is built

`PbFrame.razor` is the frame both layouts share: `PbLayout` (the public start page and sign-in, with a Navbar) and `PbWorkLayout` (the signed-in app, with a top bar and a Sidebar). Its root is `.sc-site.sc-board`, and `PbFrame.razor.css` styles the pages of both through `::deep`, including the Grid lists, bulk bars, and the header's search.

Color tokens in light mode: `--sc-primary` `#4338ca` (indigo), `--sc-primary-hover` `#3730a3`, contrast `#fff`, page background `#f6f7fb`. In dark mode (`[data-bs-theme=dark]`): `--sc-primary` `#a5b4fc`, hover `#c7d2fe`, contrast `#141a3a`, page background `#0d1020`. Because the dark primary is light, `.text-bg-primary` and `.progress-bar` text switch to the dark contrast color. The sidebar sets the `--bb-sidebar-*` variables to `#141a3a` with light text in both modes.

Fonts come from Google Fonts: Manrope (400 to 800) for body and headings, and JetBrains Mono for task keys such as `WEB-5`, hours, and amounts.

The shared stylesheet `wwwroot/css/blazorbootstrap.demo.rcl.css` maps the `--sc-*` tokens to Bootstrap (`--bs-primary`, link and focus colors, body and heading fonts, primary buttons). Charts draw on a canvas, so `PbChartTheme.cs` holds their colors: series `#4338ca`, `#047857`, `#b45309`, `#be185d`, `#0369a1` in light mode and `#a5b4fc`, `#34d399`, `#fbbf24`, `#f472b6`, `#60a5fa` in dark mode, a muted gray for targets, and axis, grid, legend, and tooltip colors. When you switch the theme, the site bar tells `PbState`, and the charts redraw.

Following the shared pattern for business roles, there is one signed-in app with a sidebar instead of role links in the public header. Pages for some roles sit behind `PbRoleGuard`, which shows a short panel to other roles, and `PbState` checks the role on every action.

## Accessibility and responsiveness

- Drag and drop always has a keyboard and single-pointer alternative: every task card has a labelled "Move to" select and Move up/down buttons, every deal card has "Move to stage" and Move up/down, and focus follows the moved card. Dragging turns off while board filters are on, and the board says why.
- Every move, save, and refusal is announced in a status region (a repeated message is announced again), and focus returns to the button that opened a dialog, or to the status message when that button is gone.
- After a failed submit, the error summary takes focus and links to each field; each message is tied to its field with `aria-describedby`.
- Each list row's checkbox is labelled with its record, and a row opens its record through a stretched link or button, so the row has no extra interactive role. Visually hidden button text keeps its leading space, so names read "Delete Grace Whitfield" rather than "DeleteGrace Whitfield".
- Each chart has a text summary and a data table. The roadmap mind map is keyboard operable (arrow keys, Enter, and Space) and is repeated as a timeline list.
- Statuses, priorities, budget warnings, and overdue dates are written in words, not shown by color alone.
- At 375 px the sidebar sits above the page, the header search wraps onto its own line, filters and bulk bars stack, the board columns scroll sideways in a labelled region, and wide grids and tables scroll inside their own box.
