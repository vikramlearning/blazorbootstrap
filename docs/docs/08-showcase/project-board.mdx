# Project & Client Board

Tandem is the project and client board of Brightline Studio, a fictitious agency. Team members work their tasks on Kanban boards, project managers run projects, milestones, workload, the roadmap, and reports, and account managers keep clients, the deal pipeline, activity, and the revenue forecast. Winning a deal creates a planned project, so the sales side and the delivery side meet in one place.

Everyone gets My tasks and Project boards. The project manager's pages and the account manager's pages are separate: an account manager doesn't see Workload or Reports, and a project manager doesn't see the pipeline. Five teammates (Kai, Amara, Ben, Lucia, and Hannah, a second account manager) appear on boards and deals but can't sign in.

All data is fictitious and there is no backend. Each browser tab keeps its own data in `sessionStorage` (it survives a reload; other tabs start fresh), and "Reset demo data" in the account menu restores the seed: 4 projects with milestones and 28 tasks, 6 clients with 9 contacts, 12 deals, and 14 activity entries. The sign-in page lists the demo accounts with one-click buttons; credentials are checked only against the tab's accounts. The clock starts on Tuesday, November 24, 2026, 9:00 and moves on with real time.

## What to try

1. Sign in as Nadia, open the WEB board, and create a task for Jordan with **New task**. Sign in as Jordan: the task is in **My tasks**, and **Mark done** moves it to Done. Back as Nadia, **Reports** counts it under "Done this week".
2. As Jordan, open the WEB board and drag a card from To do to In progress. Then do the same with the keyboard: use the card's **Move to** select, or the up and down buttons to change its place in the column.
3. Open a task, tick checklist items, post a comment, and change the assignee or status. Switch the board to **List** and sort the table by a column.
4. Sign in as Elena, open **Deal pipeline**, and move "Harbor & Pine holiday campaign" to Won with **Move to stage**. After you confirm, a planned project is created and the client's timeline logs it. Sign in as Nadia: **Manage projects** shows it under "New from a won deal", and the **Roadmap** includes it.
5. As Nadia, open **Workload**, expand a person, and reassign one of their tasks to someone with more room this week.
6. As Elena, open a client, log a call on its timeline, add a contact, and check **Forecast** for the won revenue against the $120,000 monthly target.

## How the theme is built

`PbFrame.razor` is the frame both layouts share: `PbLayout` (the public start page and sign-in, with a Navbar) and `PbWorkLayout` (the signed-in app, with a top bar and a Sidebar). Its root is `.sc-site.sc-board`, and `PbFrame.razor.css` styles the pages of both through `::deep`.

Color tokens in light mode: `--sc-primary` `#4338ca` (indigo), `--sc-primary-hover` `#3730a3`, contrast `#fff`, page background `#f6f7fb`. In dark mode (`[data-bs-theme=dark]`): `--sc-primary` `#a5b4fc`, hover `#c7d2fe`, contrast `#141a3a`, page background `#0d1020`. Because the dark primary is light, `.text-bg-primary` and `.progress-bar` text switch to the dark contrast color. The sidebar sets the `--bb-sidebar-*` variables to `#141a3a` with light text in both modes.

Fonts come from Google Fonts: Manrope (400 to 800) for body and headings, and JetBrains Mono for task keys such as `WEB-5`.

The shared stylesheet `wwwroot/css/blazorbootstrap.demo.rcl.css` maps the `--sc-*` tokens to Bootstrap (`--bs-primary`, link and focus colors, body and heading fonts, primary buttons). Charts draw on a canvas, so `PbChartTheme.cs` holds their colors: series `#4338ca`, `#047857`, `#b45309`, `#be185d`, `#0369a1` in light mode and `#a5b4fc`, `#34d399`, `#fbbf24`, `#f472b6`, `#60a5fa` in dark mode, a muted gray for targets, and axis, grid, legend, and tooltip colors. When you switch the theme, the site bar tells `PbState`, and the charts redraw.

Following the shared pattern for business roles, there is one signed-in app with a sidebar instead of role links in the public header. Pages for one role sit behind `PbRoleGuard`, which shows a short panel to other roles, and `PbState` checks the role on every action.

## Accessibility and responsiveness

- Drag and drop always has a keyboard and single-pointer alternative: every task card has a labelled "Move to" select and Move up/down buttons, every deal card has "Move to stage" and Move up/down, and focus follows the moved card. Dragging turns off while board filters are on, and the board says why.
- Every move, save, and refusal is announced in a status region, and focus returns to the button that opened a dialog.
- Each chart has a text summary and a data table. The roadmap mind map is keyboard operable (arrow keys, Enter, and Space) and is repeated as a timeline list.
- Statuses, priorities, and overdue dates are written in words, not shown by color alone.
- At 375 px the sidebar sits above the page, the board columns scroll sideways in a labelled region, and wide tables scroll.
