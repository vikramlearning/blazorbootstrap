# Help Desk & Ticketing

Beacon Desk is the support site of a fictitious software product. Customers search the knowledge base and submit and follow their requests in the help center; agents work a ticket queue with SLA timers, rich-text replies, internal notes, and saved replies, write articles, and read SLA and satisfaction reports in a separate agent desk. Admins also manage agents and teams, SLA policies, and categories.

Each browser tab keeps its own data in `sessionStorage` (it survives a reload; other tabs start fresh), and "Reset demo data" in the account menu restores the seed. The sign-in page lists the demo accounts with one-click buttons; credentials are checked only against the tab's accounts. The clock starts at November 24, 2026, 10:30 and moves on with real time, so SLA timers count down while you watch.

## What to try

1. Sign in as Ana and choose **Submit a request**. Type "two-factor" in the subject: matching articles appear under "These might help". Submit the request.
2. Switch to Jordan. The new ticket is in the queue as New. Open it, choose **Assign to me**, insert the saved reply "Thanks, looking into it", and send it. The ticket moves to Pending and its SLA shows "Paused". Switch back to Ana: **My requests** shows a "New reply" badge, and the request reads "Awaiting your reply".
3. As Ana, open the solved request #1059 and rate it with a comment. As Jordan, open **Reports › Satisfaction**: the rating and comment are in the figures and under Recent comments.
4. As Jordan, select several tickets in the queue and use the bulk bar to assign them or change their priority. Turn on **Assigned to me**, or filter by team.
5. As Jordan, open **Knowledge base › New article**, write it in the editor, and save it as a draft. Publish it, then sign out: it shows in the help center search and its category.
6. Sign in as Morgan. In **Admin › SLA policies**, lower a resolution target: the queue's overdue and at-risk counts change at once. In **Agents & teams**, deactivate Priya Nair: her open tickets go back unassigned.

## How the theme is built

`HelpDeskFrame.razor` is the frame both layouts share: `HelpDeskLayout` (help center with a Navbar) and `HelpDeskAgentLayout` (top bar with "View help center" and the account menu, plus a dark Sidebar shown only to signed-in staff). Its root is `.sc-site.sc-helpdesk`, and `HelpDeskFrame.razor.css` styles the pages of both through `::deep`.

Color tokens in light mode: `--sc-primary` `#b91c1c`, `--sc-primary-hover` `#991b1b`, contrast `#fff`, page background `#f7f7f8`. In dark mode (`[data-bs-theme=dark]`): `--sc-primary` `#f87171`, hover `#fca5a5`, contrast `#2a0a0a`, page background `#0f0f12`. Because the dark primary is a light red, `.text-bg-primary` switches to the dark contrast color. Internal notes use amber tokens (`--hd-note-bg`, `--hd-note-border`), and the agent sidebar sets the `--bb-sidebar-*` variables to `#1c1b2e` with light text and a rose badge in both modes.

Fonts come from Google Fonts: Figtree (400 to 800) for body and headings, and IBM Plex Mono (500) for ticket numbers.

The shared stylesheet `wwwroot/css/blazorbootstrap.demo.rcl.css` maps the `--sc-*` tokens to Bootstrap (`--bs-primary`, link and focus colors, body and heading fonts, primary buttons). Charts draw on a canvas, so `HelpDeskChartTheme.cs` holds their colors: teal `#0d9488` for within SLA and good ratings, orange `#ea580c` for late and bad, gray `#7c7c84` axis and legend text, and a zinc `#3f3f46` tooltip. Unlike most showcase sites, these colors are picked to read on both the light and the dark card, so the charts don't redraw when you switch the theme.

## Accessibility and responsiveness

- Search result counts, queue counts, bulk changes, and ticket actions are announced in status regions, and errors in alert regions. Focus returns to the button that opened a dialog.
- Queue rows have labelled checkboxes, and "select all" is a checkbox button with a mixed state; sortable headers carry `aria-sort`.
- Each chart sits in a wrapper with a text label (`role="img"`) and has a visually hidden data table with a caption.
- SLA states are written out ("Overdue by …", "Due in …", "Paused"), not shown by color alone.
- At 375 px the agent sidebar sits above the page (it moves beside the page from 641 px), the help center Navbar collapses into a menu, and wide tables scroll sideways.
