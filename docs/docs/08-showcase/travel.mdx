# Travel & Stay Booking

Driftwood Stays is a fictitious booking site with 18 stays in four real cities: Lisbon, Kyoto, Cape Town, and Vancouver. Guests search, compare stays on a map, book with a test card or send a request, and then change, cancel, or review their trips. Hosts work in a separate back office where they answer requests, block nights, edit listings, reply to guests and reviews, and follow their payouts.

## Roles and demo accounts

| Role | Demo account | Password | Start page |
|:--|:--|:--|:--|
| Guest | Jordan Ashdown (`jordan.ashdown@example.com`) | `stay-demo-26` | `/showcase/travel/trips` |
| Host | Aiko Tanaka (`aiko.tanaka@example.com`) | `host-demo-26` | `/showcase/travel/host` |

You can create an account on the sign-in page ("Create an account", or `?mode=register`). A new account is a guest with no trips. Aiko hosts the four Kyoto stays; the other hosts have no sign-in.

Jordan starts with an upcoming trip to Machiya Kotone, a request to Gion Lantern Inn that waits for Aiko, two completed stays (one already reviewed), two saved stays, and an unread message from Aiko. Aiko also has a second open request and one that expired unanswered.

All data is fictitious and there is no backend. Each browser tab keeps its own data in `sessionStorage` (it survives a reload; other tabs start fresh), and "Reset demo data" in the account menu restores the seed. The sign-in page lists the demo accounts with one-click buttons; credentials are checked only against the tab's accounts. The clock starts at November 24, 2026, 10:00 and moves on with real time, so trips become in progress or completed and requests expire after 24 hours.

Payments use the shared test-card form: only the published test cards are accepted (for example `4242 4242 4242 4242`), `4000 0000 0000 0002` is declined, and `4000 0000 0000 9995` fails for insufficient funds. Only the brand and last four digits are kept.

## What to try

1. Search Kyoto for your dates, filter by price or **Instant book only**, and match the numbered results to the map pins. Open a stay, look through the gallery and the availability calendar, and press **Reserve**.
2. Sign in as Jordan, pay for an instant-book stay with a test card, and see the confirmation and receipt. Try the decline card to see the error. Then use **Change dates** or **Cancel trip** on the trip page and compare the refund with the cancellation policy.
3. As Jordan, send **Request to book** for Bamboo Grove Cabin with a message. Sign in as Aiko, open the request from **Today** or **Reservations**, and accept it. Sign in as Jordan again: the trip is upcoming and shows the address.
4. As Aiko, block a few nights in **Calendar**, then open that stay's page as a guest: the nights read as unavailable and can't be booked.
5. As Jordan, use **Contact host** on a stay page. As Aiko, answer it in **Inbox** with a quick reply, then filter **Reviews** by "Needs a reply" and answer one; the reply shows on the stay page.
6. As Jordan, open the completed trip to Casa do Miradouro and post a review with the rating stars.

## Pages

### Guest site (layout: public, Navbar)

| Page | Route | What it does |
|:--|:--|:--|
| Search | `/showcase/travel` | Destination, guests, and dates; destination tiles; a signed-in guest's next trip, waiting requests, and unread messages. |
| Results | `/showcase/travel/stays` | Stays that fit the search, with price, type, sort, free-cancellation, and instant-book filters beside a map; stays booked on your dates are listed below. `?destination=` picks the city. |
| Stay | `/showcase/travel/stays/{Id:int}` | Gallery, rating, amenities, map, host with **Contact host**, policy, availability calendar, reviews, and the booking box with the price. |
| Book | `/showcase/travel/stays/{Id:int}/book` | Guests only: dates and guests, message to the host (required for a request), test-card payment, price details. Reads `?checkIn=`, `?checkOut=`, `?guests=`. |
| Trips | `/showcase/travel/trips` | Upcoming, past, and cancelled trips with their phase and what was paid. |
| Trip | `/showcase/travel/trips/{Reference}` | Details, address once confirmed, payments and refunds, and the actions for the phase: withdraw, change dates, cancel, or review. |
| Receipt | `/showcase/travel/trips/{Reference}/receipt` | A printable receipt; the site chrome doesn't print. |
| Wishlist | `/showcase/travel/wishlist` | Saved stays with the heart to remove one and **Undo**. |
| Messages | `/showcase/travel/messages` | Conversations with hosts, with unread markers. |
| Conversation | `/showcase/travel/messages/{ThreadId:int}` | Messages oldest first and a reply box with a character counter. |
| Profile | `/showcase/travel/account` | Name, email, and password. |
| Sign in | `/showcase/travel/sign-in` | Demo accounts, sign-in form, and the register form. |
| Not found | `/showcase/travel/{*path}` | The site's not-found screen, also shown for an unknown stay. |

A host who opens the guest site sees a "Viewing the site as ... (Host)" bar with **Back to hosting**; booking, the wishlist, and trips are for guests.

| Page | Route | What it does |
|:--|:--|:--|
| Today | `/showcase/travel/host` | Requests waiting, check-ins, guests staying, this month's earnings, 30-day occupancy, average rating, arrivals and departures, unread messages, and reviews without a reply. |

| Inbox | `/showcase/travel/host/inbox` | Conversations with guests about your listings. |

## How the theme is built

`TravelFrame` is shared by both layouts: `TravelLayout` (the public guest site with a Navbar) and `HostLayout` (the host back office with a Sidebar and a host-only guard), so the two sides look like one product. Its stylesheet sets the tokens on the `.sc-site.sc-travel` root and styles the pages of both sides through `::deep`.

| Token | Light | Dark |
|:--|:--|:--|
| `--sc-primary` | `#0369a1` | `#38bdf8` |
| `--sc-primary-rgb` | `3, 105, 161` | `56, 189, 248` |
| `--sc-primary-hover` | `#075985` | `#7dd3fc` |
| `--sc-primary-contrast` | `#fff` | `#082f49` |
| `--sc-page-bg` | `#f5f9fc` | `#07121b` |

The fonts are DM Sans for body text and DM Serif Display for headings, loaded from Google Fonts in the frame. The frame also defines a deep-blue hero gradient, sets dark text on primary fills in dark mode (white fails contrast on the light blue), and gives the Sidebar a navy background (`#0c2233`) through its `--bb-sidebar-*` variables. `wwwroot/css/blazorbootstrap.demo.rcl.css` bridges the `--sc-*` tokens to `--bs-primary`, link and focus colors, the body and heading fonts, and the primary buttons.

The earnings chart can't read CSS variables, so `TravelChartTheme.cs` sets its colors: paid `#0284c7`, upcoming `#ea580c`, gray axis text, and a slate tooltip. Each color is chosen to read on both the light and the dark panel, so the chart keeps the same colors when you switch theme.

## Accessibility and responsiveness

- The map placeholder has a text label, and its numbered pins match a numbered list and the numbered results; each result also names its pin for screen readers.
- Availability calendars are tables with captions where every night says in words whether it's available, unavailable, or chosen; Previous and Next announce the months shown. The host calendar switches to a list of runs of nights on narrow screens.
- The earnings chart has a summary label and a visually hidden table of the same figures.
- The gallery never moves on its own, and results counts, wishlist removals (with **Undo**), sent messages, and booking errors are announced in live regions.
- At phone width the host sidebar sits above the page, the availability calendar stacks its two months, and the results map moves below the list.
