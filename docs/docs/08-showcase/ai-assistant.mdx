# AI Assistant Workspace

Parley is the AI assistant of Halcyon Studio, a fictitious company. Members chat with simulated models whose answers stream in as Markdown, attach files, and keep a prompt library. The workspace has four roles, Owner, Admin, Prompt editor, and Member, and each one sees only the admin pages and actions it may use: Admins and the Owner manage members and roles, the model policy, spend caps, and the sensitive data review; prompt editors write and publish the shared prompts; only the Owner changes retention and hands ownership on. No model is called: replies are prewritten templates picked by keywords in your message, and they never repeat what you typed.

Every record list is a [Grid](/components/grid) with a saved view, a column chooser, and CSV export; forms validate with a linked error summary; the header has a global search and a notifications bell; and every change to roles, members, prompts, the policy, and the caps lands in an audit log. On top of that, the workspace has the governance an enterprise AI console needs: **monthly spend caps** for the workspace and for each member that warn at 80% and pause chat at 100% until an admin raises them, a **conversation retention window** that says how many conversations it will delete before it does, and **sensitive data protection** that redacts or blocks card numbers, ID numbers, bank accounts, and API keys as a message is sent, with a review queue where admins dismiss events or adjust the policy.

Each demo account has five or six notifications; the newest two are unread.

All data is fictitious and there is no backend. Each browser tab keeps its own data in `sessionStorage` (it survives a reload; other tabs start fresh), and "Reset demo data" in the account menu restores the seed. The tab stores only what changed since the seed, which is built the same way in every tab, so a fresh tab's data is about 140 bytes although it holds hundreds of records; a stored change list that doesn't fit the seed (two Owners, prompt variables that don't match the text, a sensitive data event with an unmasked value) is discarded and the seed is used. The sign-in page lists the demo accounts in role order with one-click buttons; credentials are checked only against the tab's accounts. The clock starts on November 24, 2026, 9:30 and moves on with real time.

## What to try

1. **Prompt editor.** Sign in as Priya. The navbar shows **Shared prompts** instead of **Workspace admin**, and the sidebar lists only Shared prompts. Open `/showcase/ai-assistant/admin/members` or `/showcase/ai-assistant/admin/spend`: "This page is for Admins and the Owner". In **Shared prompts**, filter **Status: Drafts**, select two, and **Publish** them: every member hears about it once. Choose **New shared prompt** and try the form's rules: the title "Meeting summary" is taken ("Checking the title…", then a message); a text with `{{audience}}` and no variables is refused ("isn't declared"); a declared `tone` the text doesn't use is refused; **Use the variables in the text** fixes it.
2. **Member.** Sign in as Sam. The bell has the new shared prompt. In **Prompts**, the Grid lists your prompts and the published shared ones; create a prompt called "Tighten my writing" (refused: you already have one).
3. **Sensitive data.** As Sam, send `Charge my card 4242 4242 4242 4242 please` in **Chat**: the message isn't sent, the status says it holds a payment card number, and **Remove it and try again** replaces it with `[card number removed]`. Send `My SSN is 123-45-6789, explain the form`: it's sent with `[ID number removed]`, and you're told. Sign in as Mei: the bell says "Sensitive data in a message". Open **Sensitive data review**: the two events are New, with the matches masked (`•••• •••• •••• 4242`). Open the card event and choose **Adjust the policy for payment card number** → **Redact**: the event is "Policy changed", and the next card number Sam sends goes through redacted. Select the other event and **Dismiss** it with a note.
4. **Spend.** As Mei, open **Spend**: the workspace bar and the members' Grid with their caps and status. Sam is at about 73% of his cap. In **Demo: use tokens**, choose Sam and press **Use 10% of their cap** once: he's at about 84%, "Warning". Sign in as Sam: the chat shows "You're near your cap" and the bell "You've used 80% of your monthly cap" (once a month). As Mei, push Sam twice more: "Blocked". As Sam, any message is refused: "you've used your monthly cap… Ask an Admin or Alex Morgan (the Owner) to raise it". As Mei, open Sam's row: a cap at or below what he used is refused; set one higher, and Sam can chat again ("Your cap was raised: you can chat again"). Press **Use 5% of the workspace cap** until the workspace passes 80% (Mei and Alex are told) and then 100%: everyone's chat pauses. Raising the workspace cap below what's used is refused; above it, chat is open again and every member is told.
5. **Retention.** Sign in as Alex. In **Models and policy**, choose **90 days**: "24 conversations of 4 members older than 90 days will be deleted when you save". Save, confirm in the dialog, and the conversations are gone; each member whose conversations went gets one notice, and the audit log has the count. **Keep forever** deletes nothing. As Mei, retention is read-only.
6. **Roles.** As Alex, open **Members and roles**: the "Roles and permissions" panel shows the matrix. Promote Sam to Prompt editor (Sam is told); your own row says nobody changes their own role, and Owner isn't in the role list. Transfer ownership to Mei after typing the workspace name: you're an Admin now, and Retention is read-only for you.
7. **Audit log.** As Alex or Mei, open **Audit log**: every step above is on top, with the cap changes, the demo usage, the sensitive data events with masked matches only, the reviews, and retention with its count.
8. **Search.** As Mei, search `sam` in the header: Sam's membership opens the members list filtered to him; search a prompt title or one of your conversations. As Sam, the search finds only prompts and your own conversations.

## Roles and permissions

| Permission | Member | Prompt editor | Admin | Owner |
|:--|:--:|:--:|:--:|:--:|
| Chat, keep personal prompts, and see their own usage | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Write, publish, and delete shared prompts | | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Usage analytics, members and roles (up to Admin), settings and model policy, spend caps, sensitive data review, audit log | | | Yes | Yes |
| Retention and transferring ownership | | | | Yes |

The workspace has exactly one Owner, who is active and can sign in. Nobody changes their own role or access, nobody grants Owner by a role change, and nobody changes or removes the Owner. Ownership moves only by a transfer: the Owner picks an active member who can sign in, types the workspace name to confirm, and confirms again in a dialog; the old Owner becomes an Admin. "Remove access" deactivates a member (their conversations are kept and access can be restored) and withdraws an invitation. `AiState` checks every rule on every action, so a page or a bulk bar can't get around it, and each refusal is said in words. Role changes, access changes, and transfers are audited, and the person they're about is told when they can sign in.

## Governance

**Audit log.** Invitations, joins, role and access changes, ownership transfers, shared prompts (created, edited, published, unpublished, deleted), the workspace name, the model policy, retention and what it deleted, spend caps and the demo control's usage, the sensitive data policy, its events, and their reviews. Entries name what changed with lengths and counts; chat content and prompt text are never copied, and a sensitive match appears only masked. Personal prompts are private and aren't audited.

**Spend caps.** Caps count tokens, the unit the site already uses (about four characters each, for messages and replies); there are no prices. The workspace has a monthly cap for every member together, every member has the default member cap unless an admin gives them their own, and a member's cap can't be above the workspace's. At 80% of a cap the chat shows a warning in words for that scope and the person hears about it once a month (the member about their own cap, the Owner and Admins about the workspace's). At 100% the state refuses to send, for that member or, for the workspace cap, for everyone, until an admin raises the cap above what is already used; raising it unblocks at once and tells whoever it unblocked. A new cap must be above what was used this month; the default can't be above the workspace cap, and the workspace cap can't be below a member's own cap. "Demo: use tokens" on Spend adds usage quickly (10% of a member's cap, or 5% of the workspace cap spread over the members without a sign-in) so you can watch the thresholds. Caps start again on the 1st of each month.

**Retention.** The Owner chooses Keep forever, 30, 90, or 365 days, or a custom number of days. Before saving, the page says how many conversations of how many members are older than the window; saving asks to confirm, deletes them, and tells each member whose conversations went once. The window keeps applying as time passes. Keep forever deletes nothing.

## Sensitive data protection

The policy has four patterns, each off, redact, or block:

| Pattern | Matches | Starts as |
|:--|:--|:--|
| Payment card number | 13 to 19 digits, spaces or dashes allowed, that pass the Luhn check, like `4242 4242 4242 4242` | Block |
| National ID (SSN) | A US Social Security number like `123-45-6789`, without the impossible ranges (000, 666, 900 and up, a 00 group, a 0000 serial) | Redact |
| Bank account (IBAN) | Two letters, two check digits, and the account, like `DE89 3704 0044 0532 0130 00`, when the check digits are right | Redact |
| API key | `sk-` and 20 or more letters, digits, dashes, or underscores | Block |

When a member sends a message, a match the policy blocks stops it: nothing is sent or kept, the member is told which kind of data it held (never the data), and **Remove it and try again** puts the message back with placeholders. A match the policy redacts is replaced (like `[card number removed]`) before the message, its title, or its usage is kept, and the member is told. The assistant's replies are templates and never repeat the message. Each match goes to the **Sensitive data review** queue with the member, the pattern, what was done, the conversation's title (it comes from the redacted text), and the match masked to its last four characters. Admins and the Owner hear about new events (one notice a minute at most), dismiss them one at a time or in bulk with a note, or adjust the pattern from the review, which marks the event "Policy changed". Attachment names aren't checked.

## Enterprise features

- **Grid lists**: members and invitations, shared prompts, the prompt library, spend, sensitive data events, and the audit log use the [Grid](/components/grid) with sorting, paging, a column chooser, and CSV export of the filtered rows (`ai-members`, `ai-shared-prompts`, `ai-prompt-library`, `ai-spend`, `ai-sensitive-data`, `ai-audit-log`). Each list keeps its view (sort, page size, hidden and reordered columns) in the tab's `sessionStorage` until **Reset view**. A whole row opens its record, or its dialog when it has no page of its own. Members, shared prompts, the prompt library, spend, and sensitive data have multi-row selection with a bulk bar.
- **Validated forms**: invitations, the prompt forms, settings, retention, spend caps, a member's cap, the bulk bars, the sensitive data policy and reviews, ownership transfer, and preferences use `EditForm`. Required, length, and format rules, uniqueness checks with a "Checking…" state (an invitation's email; a prompt's title in its scope), ranges, cross-field rules (variables against the text, the default model among the models that are on, a file size while attachments are on, a role up to what you may give, caps against the workspace cap and against what's used, a review that changes the policy), and a summary that links each failing field after a failed submit.
- **Global search** in the header: everyone finds prompts in their scope and their own conversations; prompt editors also find drafts; Admins and the Owner also find members and invitations.
- **Notifications** with an unread count and mark as read: role and access changes, shared prompts published, a prompt you wrote edited or deleted by someone else, ownership transfers, joins, an admin's policy change (to the Owner), spend warnings and blocks, caps raised, conversations deleted by retention, and sensitive data events.
- **Audit log** of everything under [Governance](#governance).
- **Loading, empty, and error states** on every list and on the usage charts, with a "Demo: simulate a loading error" control to show the error state and its Retry.

## How the theme is built

`AiFrame.razor` is the frame both layouts share: `AiLayout` (members' app with a Navbar, the search, and the bell) and `AiAdminLayout` (top bar with the search, the bell, "Back to chat", and a dark violet Sidebar). Its root is `.sc-site.sc-ai`, and `AiFrame.razor.css` styles the pages of both through `::deep`, including the Grid lists and bulk bars.

Color tokens in light mode: `--sc-primary` `#6d28d9`, `--sc-primary-hover` `#5b21b6`, contrast `#fff`, page background `#f7f5fc`. In dark mode (`[data-bs-theme=dark]`): `--sc-primary` `#c4b5fd`, hover `#ddd6fe`, contrast `#1e1338`, page background `#0f0c18`. Because the dark primary is a light violet, `.text-bg-primary` and `.progress-bar` text switch to the dark contrast color. The admin sidebar sets the `--bb-sidebar-*` variables to `#1e1338` with light text in both modes.

Fonts come from Google Fonts: Plus Jakarta Sans (400 to 700) for body and headings, and JetBrains Mono for code blocks, figures, and masked matches.

The shared stylesheet `wwwroot/css/blazorbootstrap.demo.rcl.css` maps the `--sc-*` tokens to Bootstrap (`--bs-primary`, link and focus colors, body and heading fonts, primary buttons). Charts draw on a canvas, so `AiChartTheme.cs` holds their colors: series `#6d28d9`, `#2563eb`, `#059669`, `#b45309`, `#be185d` in light mode and `#c4b5fd`, `#60a5fa`, `#34d399`, `#fbbf24`, `#f472b6` in dark mode, plus axis, grid, legend, and tooltip colors. When you switch the theme, the site bar tells `AiState`, and the charts redraw.

## Accessibility and responsiveness

- Streamed text is not read aloud token by token: a status region says once when a reply starts and once when it ends, and the reply is `aria-busy` while it streams. When Stop disappears, focus moves to the rating buttons.
- Spend warnings and blocks are status regions with the share and what to do, in words and with an icon, not color alone; statuses in the lists are words in badges.
- After a failed submit, the error summary takes focus and links to each field; each message is tied to its field with `aria-describedby`. A refused message says why and keeps the text in the box.
- Every outcome is announced in a status region (a repeated message is announced again), and focus returns to the button that opened a dialog, or to the status message when that button is gone. When your role changes while a page is open, focus moves to the page's "This page is for …" heading.
- Each list row's checkbox is labelled with its record, and a row opens its record through a stretched link or button, so the row has no extra interactive role.
- Each chart has a text summary and a data table with a caption.
- At 375 px the conversation list moves into an Offcanvas drawer, the admin sidebar sits above the page, the header search wraps onto its own line, filters and bulk bars stack, and wide grids scroll inside their own box.
