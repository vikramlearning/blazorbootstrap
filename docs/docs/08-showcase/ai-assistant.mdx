# AI Assistant Workspace

Parley is the AI assistant of Halcyon Studio, a fictitious company. Members chat with simulated models whose answers stream in as Markdown, attach files, and keep a prompt library; workspace admins manage members, shared prompts, usage, and the model policy in a separate back office. No model is called: replies are prewritten templates picked by keywords in your message.

## Roles and demo accounts

| Role | Demo account | Password | Start page |
|:--|:--|:--|:--|
| Member | Sam Rivera (`sam.rivera@example.com`) | `chat-demo-26` | `/showcase/ai-assistant/chat` |
| Workspace admin | Alex Morgan (`alex.morgan@example.com`) | `chat-admin-26` | `/showcase/ai-assistant/admin` |

You can also create an account ("Join the workspace" on the start page). A new account joins as an active member. If you register with the email of an open invitation (the seed has one for `nora.kim@example.com`), the account takes up that invitation and its role.

Access comes from the workspace membership, not the sign-in role: an admin can promote, demote, or deactivate members in the tab, and the pages follow at once.

All data is fictitious and there is no backend. Each browser tab keeps its own data in `sessionStorage` (it survives a reload; other tabs start fresh), and "Reset demo data" in the account menu restores the seed. The sign-in page lists the demo accounts with one-click buttons; credentials are checked only against the tab's accounts. The clock starts at November 24, 2026, 9:30 and moves on with real time.

## What to try

1. Sign in as Sam, open **Chat**, and pick a starter such as "Write a SQL query". Watch the reply stream in, press **Stop** on another one, then rate a reply and press **Regenerate**.
2. In a conversation, choose **Prompts** to insert a saved prompt, attach a file (only its name and size are kept), and send. Then open **Usage** to see the new tokens against the monthly quota.
3. Open **Prompts**, create a prompt with tags, and use **Use in chat** to put it in the message box.
4. Sign in as Alex. In **Models and policy**, turn off Parley Swift, lower the monthly quota, or add a blocked term. Sign in as Sam again: the chat shows the model notice, refuses messages over the quota, and answers a message with a blocked term with a policy refusal.
5. As Alex, open **Members and roles**: invite someone, promote Sam to admin (Sam's header then shows **Workspace admin**), or deactivate Sam (Sam's chat then says access was removed).

- [Modal](/components/modal): rename and delete a conversation, insert a saved prompt, delete a prompt, member changes, and the retention warning.

- [Progress](/components/progress): the quota bar on Usage and each top member's share on Usage analytics.
- [Breadcrumb](/components/breadcrumb): the prompt editors.
- [Badge](/components/badge): member status in the members table.

## How the theme is built

`AiFrame.razor` is the frame both layouts share: `AiLayout` (members' app with a Navbar) and `AiAdminLayout` (top bar with "Back to chat" and a dark violet Sidebar). Its root is `.sc-site.sc-ai`, and `AiFrame.razor.css` styles the pages of both through `::deep`.

Color tokens in light mode: `--sc-primary` `#6d28d9`, `--sc-primary-hover` `#5b21b6`, contrast `#fff`, page background `#f7f5fc`. In dark mode (`[data-bs-theme=dark]`): `--sc-primary` `#c4b5fd`, hover `#ddd6fe`, contrast `#1e1338`, page background `#0f0c18`. Because the dark primary is a light violet, `.text-bg-primary` and `.progress-bar` text switch to the dark contrast color. The admin sidebar sets the `--bb-sidebar-*` variables to `#1e1338` with light text in both modes.

Fonts come from Google Fonts: Plus Jakarta Sans (400 to 700) for body and headings, and JetBrains Mono for code blocks and figures.

The shared stylesheet `wwwroot/css/blazorbootstrap.demo.rcl.css` maps the `--sc-*` tokens to Bootstrap (`--bs-primary`, link and focus colors, body and heading fonts, primary buttons). Charts draw on a canvas, so `AiChartTheme.cs` holds their colors: series `#6d28d9`, `#2563eb`, `#059669`, `#b45309`, `#be185d` in light mode and `#c4b5fd`, `#60a5fa`, `#34d399`, `#fbbf24`, `#f472b6` in dark mode, plus axis, grid, legend, and tooltip colors. When you switch the theme, the site bar tells `AiState`, and the charts redraw.

## Accessibility and responsiveness

- Streamed text is not read aloud token by token: a status region says once when a reply starts and once when it ends, and the reply is `aria-busy` while it streams. When Stop disappears, focus moves to the rating buttons.
- Every outcome (sent, refused and why, saved, deleted) is announced in a status region, and focus returns to the button that opened a dialog, or to the status message when that button is gone.
- Each chart has a text summary and a data table with a caption.
- Enter sends and Shift+Enter adds a line (Ctrl+Enter or ⌘+Enter when Enter-to-send is off in the profile); the pin toggle uses `aria-pressed`.
- At 375 px the conversation list moves into an Offcanvas drawer, the admin sidebar sits above the page, and wide tables scroll sideways.
