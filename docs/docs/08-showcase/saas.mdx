# SaaS Product

Lumora is a fictitious product analytics service. The site has three parts: a public marketing site with pricing and a trial sign-up, the workspace a customer signs in to (usage, team, billing, settings), and the console Lumora's operators use to run the business (tenants, leads, invoices, plans, revenue). The parts share one set of data, so a change in one shows up in the others.

## Roles and demo accounts

| Role | Demo account | Password | Start page |
|:--|:--|:--|:--|
| Customer | Amara Okafor (`amara@northwind.example`) | `lumora-demo-26` | `/showcase/saas/app` |
| Operator | Sam Ortega (`sam@lumora.example`) | `ops-demo-26` | `/showcase/saas/admin` |

Amara owns the Northwind Outfitters workspace (Growth plan, billed annually, 12 seats). Its team has an admin, a billing member, members, and one open invitation; only Amara can sign in.

There is no separate register form: at Lumora an account comes with a workspace, so "Create an account" opens the trial sign-up. A new account becomes the owner of a new workspace: Growth starts a 14-day trial without a card, and Starter is free. Enterprise is sold through "Contact sales".

All data is fictitious and there is no backend. Each browser tab keeps its own data in `sessionStorage` (it survives a reload; other tabs start fresh), and "Reset demo data" in the account menu restores the seed. The sign-in page lists the demo accounts with one-click buttons; credentials are checked only against the tab's accounts. The clock starts at November 9, 2026, 9:00 and moves on with real time. Card forms accept only the published test cards (for example `4242 4242 4242 4242`; `4000 0000 0000 0002` is declined).

## What to try

1. Start a trial from **Pricing** or **Start free trial**: pick Growth, fill in your name, company, work email, and a password, and you land in your new workspace with a trial banner. On **Billing**, choose a plan, add a test card, and convert the trial.
2. Sign in as Amara. On **Team**, invite a teammate and change a member's role. On **Billing**, change the seats or switch to monthly and read the prorated quote before you confirm.
3. As a visitor, send a message from **Contact sales**. Sign in as Sam: the message is in **Leads**. Open it, set its status, and use the link to create a tenant prefilled from the lead.
4. As Sam, open **Plans & prices** and change the Growth price. The public **Pricing** page, the sign-up, and Billing show the new price at once.
5. As Sam, open the Northwind Outfitters tenant and suspend it, or refund one of its invoices. Sign in as Amara: the workspace shows the suspension (changes are turned off) and a new notification.

## How the theme is built

This is one of the older sites, so there is no shared frame component. The public site's layout, `SaasLayout.razor`, has the root `.sc-site.sc-lumora`. The workspace (`SaasAppLayout`) and the console (`SaasAdminLayout`) both render `SaasShell.razor`, the app shell with the Sidebar and a top bar; its root is `.sc-site.sc-saas`, and `SaasShell.razor.css` styles the pages of both through `::deep`. Each part keeps its own layout, so the public site never shows the app's sidebar (D-031).

Both stylesheets set the same brand tokens. Light mode: `--sc-primary` `#c026d3` (magenta), `--sc-primary-hover` `#a21caf`, contrast `#fff`. Dark mode (`[data-bs-theme=dark]`): `--sc-primary` `#e879f9`, hover `#f0abfc`, contrast `#2e1065`. The app shell adds a dark purple sidebar (`#1e1033`, `#120a1f` in dark mode) and page background `#f4f6fb` (`#0b1220` in dark mode).

Fonts come from Google Fonts: Sora (600 to 800) for headings and Inter for body text; the app shell also loads JetBrains Mono for figures and invoice numbers.

The shared stylesheet `wwwroot/css/blazorbootstrap.demo.rcl.css` maps the `--sc-*` tokens to Bootstrap (`--bs-primary`, link and focus colors, body and heading fonts, primary buttons). Charts draw on a canvas, so `SaasChartTheme.cs` holds their colors: series `#c026d3`, `#2563eb`, `#059669`, `#b45309`, `#7c3aed` in light mode and `#e879f9`, `#60a5fa`, `#34d399`, `#fbbf24`, `#a78bfa` in dark mode, a muted gray for targets, and axis, grid, legend, and tooltip colors. When you switch the theme, the app shell's site bar tells `SaasState`, and the charts redraw.

## Accessibility and responsiveness

- Each chart has a summary sentence and a visually hidden data table.
- Every action on Team, Billing, Settings, and the console ends in a status message or an error; a repeated message is announced again. Focus returns to the button that opened a dialog, or to the status message when that button is gone.
- Statuses (trial, past due, suspended, paid, refunded) are written as words in badges, not shown by color alone.
- At 375 px the tenant table folds its columns into the tenant cell and a "Sort by" list replaces the column headers; the public Navbar collapses into a menu.
