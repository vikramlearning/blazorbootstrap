# SaaS Product

Lumora is a fictitious product analytics service. The site has three parts: a public marketing site with pricing and a trial sign-up, the workspace a customer signs in to (usage, team, billing, settings), and the console Lumora's operators use to run the business (tenants, leads, invoices, dunning, plans, revenue). The parts share one set of data, so a change in one shows up in the others.

Every record list in the workspace and the console is a [Grid](/components/grid) with a saved view, a column chooser, and CSV export; forms validate with a linked error summary; and both headers have a global search and notifications. Every change lands in an audit log. The signature workflow is **dunning**: when a charge fails, Lumora retries it on a schedule, then gives the customer a grace period, then suspends the workspace; the customer sees a payment-failed banner and updates the card, and a successful payment recovers the tenant. Operators follow every failed payment on a Dunning page and can retry it, extend the grace period, or suspend early.

   4. Move the clock forward one day at a time (from Billing, or Sam's Dunning page). On the 1st, 3rd, and 7th day the retries fail, and each one notifies Amara. ## Dunning

**The policy.** Retry the failed charge on 1 to 5 days after the failure (each from day 1 to day 30, each later than the one before), then give 0 to 30 days of grace after the last failed retry, then suspend the workspace. The seed's policy is days 1, 3, and 7 and 7 days of grace. "Email the customer" is a label in this demo: billing contacts get in-app notifications either way. A policy change applies to charges that fail from then on; open cases keep the schedule they started with. The editor previews the policy in words and explains what is wrong ("List the retry days in order, each later than the one before (like 1, 3, 7)").

**When a charge fails.** A renewal, a trial's end, or a payment someone tries fails when the workspace has no card, or its card fails (expired, declined, or insufficient funds). The invoice stays open, the workspace becomes past due, and a case starts with the first retry scheduled. A workspace in dunning doesn't renew again until it recovers.

**The stages.**

1. **Retrying**: each scheduled retry charges the card on file. It goes through once the customer has saved a working card; otherwise it fails with the same reason, and the next retry is scheduled.
2. **In grace**: after the last failed retry, the workspace stays usable (past due) until the grace period ends.
3. **Suspended**: when grace ends, the workspace is suspended: its members can't change anything, but owners and billing members can still update the card and pay.
4. **Recovered**: a successful payment (a retry, an operator's **Retry now**, or the customer's **Pay now**) pays the invoice, and the workspace is active again.

**What operators can do.** **Retry now** charges the card at once (it doesn't change the schedule). **Extend grace** adds 1 to 14 days with a required reason; a suspended workspace becomes usable again until the new end. **Suspend now** suspends a retrying or grace case early, with a reason. Making a dunning-suspended tenant active again in the tenant editor or the bulk bar gives it a new grace period. **Demo: simulate a failed charge** charges an active tenant's next renewal now with a declining card, to start dunning on a tenant in good standing.

**The engine** runs on the site's clock whenever billing catches up: when a workspace or console page opens, when a tab's data is restored, and when the demo clock moves a day. Steps that came due while nobody looked run in time order, with their due times.

**Notifications.** The workspace's owners, admins, and billing members who can sign in are told when the charge fails, at each failed retry, when grace starts, when an operator extends it, at the suspension, and at the recovery. Lumora's operators are told when a charge fails, when a tenant enters grace, is suspended, or recovers, and when a customer in dunning updates the card.

**Audit.** Every step is recorded: "Payment failed", each "Payment retry failed" (by automatic billing, an operator, or the customer), "Grace period started", "Grace period extended" (with the reason), "Tenant suspended", "Tenant reactivated", "Payment method updated" (brand and last four digits only), "Payment recovered", "Failed charge simulated", and "Dunning policy updated".

## Enterprise features

- **Grid lists**: tenants, invoices, dunning cases, leads, the audit log, a tenant's members and invoices, the customer's team, and the billing history use the [Grid](/components/grid) with sorting, paging, a column chooser, and CSV export of the filtered rows. Each list keeps its view (sort, page size, hidden and reordered columns) in the tab's `sessionStorage` until **Reset view**. A whole row opens its record (or, without a page of its own, its dialog). The tenant, invoice, lead, and dunning lists have multi-row selection with a bulk bar.
- **Validated forms**: required, length, and format rules (emails, subdomains, card numbers with the Luhn check, expiry, security code, postal code), uniqueness checks with a "Checking…" state (tenant name and subdomain, plan name, an open lead's email, a teammate's email, an account's email, the workspace name), cross-field rules (seats within the plan, Most popular only on a self-serve plan, a closed lead's note, an EU workspace's data region, a password that doesn't contain the email, a dunning schedule in order), and a summary that links each failing field after a failed submit.
- **Global search** in both headers: operators find tenants (name, subdomain, owner), leads, invoices, and plans; customers find their team and their invoices.
- **Notifications** with an unread count and mark as read: for operators, new sign-ups and leads, failed payments and dunning steps, tenants out of seats, and tenants they now manage; for customers, dunning steps, payments and refunds, plan changes by Lumora, renewals, and accepted invitations.
- **Audit log** of operators (tenants, plans, leads, refunds, payments, dunning), automatic billing (renewals, failed charges, retries, suspensions), and customers (sign-ups, plan and payment changes, team changes, settings).
- **Loading, empty, and error states** on every list, with a "Demo: simulate a loading error" control to show the error state and its Retry.

## How the theme is built

This is one of the older sites, so there is no shared frame component. The public site's layout, `SaasLayout.razor`, has the root `.sc-site.sc-lumora`. The workspace (`SaasAppLayout`) and the console (`SaasAdminLayout`) both render `SaasShell.razor`, the app shell with the Sidebar and a top bar (the global search, the site link, the notifications, and the account menu); its root is `.sc-site.sc-saas`, and `SaasShell.razor.css` styles the pages of both through `::deep`. Each part keeps its own layout, so the public site never shows the app's sidebar (D-031).

Both stylesheets set the same brand tokens. Light mode: `--sc-primary` `#c026d3` (magenta), `--sc-primary-hover` `#a21caf`, contrast `#fff`. Dark mode (`[data-bs-theme=dark]`): `--sc-primary` `#e879f9`, hover `#f0abfc`, contrast `#2e1065`. The app shell adds a dark purple sidebar (`#1e1033`, `#120a1f` in dark mode) and page background `#f4f6fb` (`#0b1220` in dark mode).

Fonts come from Google Fonts: Sora (600 to 800) for headings and Inter for body text; the app shell also loads JetBrains Mono for figures and invoice numbers.

The shared stylesheet `wwwroot/css/blazorbootstrap.demo.rcl.css` maps the `--sc-*` tokens to Bootstrap (`--bs-primary`, link and focus colors, body and heading fonts, primary buttons). Charts draw on a canvas, so `SaasChartTheme.cs` holds their colors: series `#c026d3`, `#2563eb`, `#059669`, `#b45309`, `#7c3aed` in light mode and `#e879f9`, `#60a5fa`, `#34d399`, `#fbbf24`, `#a78bfa` in dark mode, a muted gray for targets, and axis, grid, legend, and tooltip colors. When you switch the theme, the app shell's site bar tells `SaasState`, and the charts redraw.

## Accessibility and responsiveness

- Each chart has a summary sentence and a visually hidden data table.
- Every action ends in a status message or an error; a repeated message is announced again. Focus returns to the button that opened a dialog, or to the status message when that button is gone.
- Statuses and dunning stages (trial, past due, suspended, retrying, in grace, recovered, paid, refunded) are written as words in badges, not shown by color alone. The payment-failed banner is an alert with an icon and a sentence that names the amount and the next date.
- Each list row's checkbox is labelled with its record ("Select Northwind Outfitters"), and the header checkbox reads "Select all shown tenants". A row opens its record through a stretched link or button, so the row has no extra interactive role.
- After a failed submit, the error summary takes focus and links to each field; each message is tied to its field with `aria-describedby`. Visually hidden button text keeps its leading space, so names read "Remove Jonah Pike" rather than "RemoveJonah Pike".
- At 375 px, filters and bulk bars stack, the header search wraps onto its own line, wide grids scroll inside their own box, and the public Navbar collapses into a menu.
